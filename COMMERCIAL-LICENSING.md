# Commercial licensing

KodeWaspada is publicly available under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). That public license permits noncommercial use, modification, and distribution under its terms. It does not grant commercial-use rights.

A separate paid commercial license can be requested from the copyright holder for a defined use case. This is a dual-licensing model: the public noncommercial license remains available, while an accepted buyer receives additional rights through a separate written agreement.

## When to request a commercial license

Request a quote before using KodeWaspada for activities such as:

- internal business operations at a for-profit organization;
- paid client work or a commercial security-review service;
- integration into a paid product, platform, or subscription;
- redistribution, embedding, white-labeling, or resale in a commercial offering;
- another use where commercial permission is required or uncertain.

## What the agreement can define

Each quote is scoped to the buyer's actual use. The written agreement can define:

- the licensed individual, company, or affiliated entities;
- permitted internal, client-service, hosted, embedded, or redistribution use;
- covered versions, users, installations, products, and territories;
- term, fee, payment schedule, renewal, and termination;
- whether updates, support, customization, or an SLA are included.

No fixed public price is promised. A quote depends on the requested rights and support. Buying a commercial license does not transfer KodeWaspada's copyright or grant exclusivity unless a separate signed agreement expressly says so.

## Start an inquiry

[Open a commercial-license inquiry](https://github.com/gustiakmal52/kodewaspada/issues/new?template=commercial-license.yml) using only information safe to publish. The issue is an initial, non-binding contact point; do not post private source code, customer information, credentials, legal identity documents, payment details, or confidential contract terms. The parties can agree on a private channel before exchanging sensitive information.

Submitting an inquiry, receiving a quote, or making an unsolicited payment does not grant commercial rights. Commercial permission begins only after the copyright holder and buyer execute a written agreement and any agreed payment condition is satisfied.

## Boundaries

- The agreement covers only the original KodeWaspada material identified in it.
